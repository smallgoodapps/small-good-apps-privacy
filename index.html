Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

MB to KB: Image Compressor is developed by Small Good Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Image Processing

Images selected for compression are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your images to our own servers.

Depending on the option you choose, image metadata such as EXIF information may be preserved or removed during compression.

File Access

The app accesses only images and folders that you explicitly select using Android's system file and folder pickers.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

Where required by applicable law, consent options may be shown before personalized advertising is used.

Crash Reporting

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes and improve app stability.

Crash reports may include technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic information.

Firebase Crashlytics does not receive the images you compress through this app.

Data Storage

Compressed images are saved only to locations selected by you on your device. We do not operate a cloud storage service for your images.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app or the services it uses change. The latest version will always be available at this page.
